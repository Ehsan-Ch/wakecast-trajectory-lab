Read the research protocol

Observe recent motion

Origins require reported speed ≥2 knots. Estimate velocity from the earliest position in the past six minutes to the origin, with at least two minutes of history.

Keep time in order

Use an observed endpoint nearest ten minutes, within ±60 seconds. Forecast at that endpoint’s actual 9–11 minute lead. No coordinates after the origin enter the motion estimate.

Respect missing evidence

Reject windows with report gaps over three minutes and labels crossing a split boundary. This excludes missing and out-of-area futures; results apply only to the retained cases.

Expose uncertainty

Show every model, including a stationary baseline. Report measured coverage without assuming independent cases or a calibrated operational safety guarantee.

The observations behind the forecast.
